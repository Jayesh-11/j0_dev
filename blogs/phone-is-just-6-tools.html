Phone is just 6 tools

Phone has very few use cases and it should be used as a tool: I think which is what Steve Jobs also intended it to be in THE iPhone introduction, except we update the list now

  1. Call/Emergency
  2. Camera/Video recorder
  3. Payments
  4. Navigation
  5. Flashlight
  6. Music Player
Hey, bonus if your phone is NOT an explosive.

You will see a pattern here—all the phone is a swiss knife of really useful hardware, plus it's really useful when you travel outside with these tools.

Before we go any further, I am not married to the idea of just these 6 tools, give plus minus 2, and from what I have seen most people don't use it for anything more than this.

There can be other use cases which can be industry specific, like being connected to colleagues, someone in music industry, taking notes, voice record or whatever(do your own plus minus 2). As a software developer never crossed these use cases.

Communication is there but use case is pretty subjective and also I don't like the idea people messaging on phone work or personal, you want to connect, CALL or if you are someone I don't know I'll check my email. Point is use it as a tool(your use cases might vary), use it when needed and keep it away once its done, don't jump between looking for things to do.

It's okay to be limited in that sense — I don't want my phone to do everything.

I want my phone to do these things REALLY well. I can't focus on this point enough.

My Experience with Nothing Phone 1

Currently I have Nothing phone 1. I got this a year after it was launched, a 2023 purchase. Checked all my boxes (I was just looking for a good enough camera) and wanted to take risk with a new company, which I will do again but not Nothing.

Once my phone fell, the front camera, which is now used by many payments app to verify if it's a real user, stopped working. Now my options were limited. I went to Nothing service center, they replaced the camera module, since it was under warranty.

Every once in a while—about every 2-3 months—front camera stops working. I visited the service center like 4 times, and every time they fixed it. Post that I just gave up. I figured out if I dropped my phone at right angle camera works again. Initially it bothered me, but now I just never really use front camera.

I do understand it's difficult to get tech right. As a web developer it's painful to get it right. What I am talking about here is doing things right after things have gone bad. 4 visits to service center for same issue is bad. Maybe it's just about one location—service center is bad: Bangalore, India.

The Contrast: Mi Note 4

I have a Mi Note 4 (half the price of Phone 1), which I got in 2017, which still works fine (checklist), except the screen is cracked and some payment apps have stopped supporting the android version. But that phone has fallen, drowned, thrown, sat on and gotten lost. Despite all that works really well. Not a single visit to service center.

But since some apps refuse to support old android phones, I can't use that.

The Alternative: Used iPhone X

I got a used iPhone X for content creation, which can take better photos than Phone 1. But using that only for content creation. For 10,000 INR / around $120, it's a better value product than Phone 1.

My Recommendation

I'll suggest to invest in really reliable device or at least one with better post services.

I'll use Phone 1 until it lasts, or maybe I'll just sell this one and get used iPhone X.
